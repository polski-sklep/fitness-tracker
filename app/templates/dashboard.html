Power Index

Reading the score

A Power Index of 100 represents your typical session. Above 130 is a strong session, below 70 is a weak one. The all-time ceiling so far is 262 (March 16). The number is computed against a personal median of 3,161 raw points across 29 sessions.

Per-exercise score

Each exercise contributes a score equal to:

Volume × ExerciseClass × RelativeIntensity × Effort × LocalSensitivity ÷ ReadinessTerm

  • Volume: sets × reps × weight. Bodyweight movements assume 78 kg.
  • ExerciseClass: a coefficient by movement type. Heavy compound lifts score 1.00; bodyweight 0.95; dumbbell 0.92; machine 0.85; isolation 0.70; corrective 0.55.
  • RelativeIntensity: how heavy the set was relative to your peak estimated 1RM for that exercise.
  • Effort: a multiplier based on reps-in-reserve. Peaks at RIR 1, drops at failure (RIR 0) and rises sharply when you leave too much in the tank (RIR 3+).
  • LocalSensitivity: penalty when an exercise aggravates a specific joint. None = 1.00, mild = 0.85, moderate = 0.65, severe = 0.40.
  • ReadinessTerm: divides by (R/10)^0.4. Same output on a low-readiness day earns more credit than on a high-readiness day.

Per-session score

The exercise scores sum, then multiply by four session-level modifiers:

Total = sum(ExerciseScores) × Quality × Fatigue × SessionSensitivity × Time

  • Quality: (Q/10)^1.2. High quality multiplies; low quality divides.
  • Fatigue: a concave curve peaking around RIR 1 equivalent. Both undertraining and overreaching cost points.
  • SessionSensitivity: piecewise penalty. Above 9 is full credit; 5-7 mild penalty; below 5 sharp penalty.
  • Time: ratio of benchmark duration to actual, raised to 0.4. Going faster than benchmark earns slight bonus; going much longer costs slightly.

Time benchmarks

Lower body + core: 94.5 min. Upper pull: 77.0 min. Upper push: 81.0 min.

What this is and isn't

The formula is a personal index, not a scientifically validated stimulus model. The coefficients are reasoned defaults, not derived from your hypertrophy outcomes. After 3-6 months of clean per-set RIR logging plus body composition data, the parameters could be regression-fitted to actual results. Until then: directionally accurate, magnitude approximate.
